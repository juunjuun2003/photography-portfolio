About the photographer

Quietly
observant.

I am Thien, a photographer and storyteller based in Fredericton, New Brunswick. I photograph people with an editorial eye and a documentary instinct.

I look for gesture, light, relationships, and the small details that make a day feel like itself. The goal is not to make a moment look perfect. It is to preserve why it mattered.

Field notes

What I look
for.

A few principles behind the photographs: simple ideas that shape how I watch, wait, and arrange a visual story.

01

The gesture before the pose.

The photograph often begins one second before someone believes they are ready.

02

The room is part of the portrait.

Architecture, weather, light, and distance help explain who was there and how the day felt.

03

Taste is a form of restraint.

A complete story does not need every photograph. It needs the right photographs in the right order.
